# Project M1 — Charter

## Mission

Develop a commercially competitive electric traction motor for the Indian market, starting with
evidence-based research, a reproducible simulation baseline, and a small experimental prototype.

## Objectives

1. Research magnet-free and alternative traction architectures (SRM, SynRM, PMSM benchmark, induction).
2. Identify a measurable technical opportunity grounded in published evidence.
3. Establish a reproducible simulation and design-optimization workflow.
4. Investigate AI-assisted motor optimization with scientific computing.
5. Design and validate a ~0.5–1 kW experimental prototype on a 24–48 V test platform.
6. Assess feasibility for Indian electric two-wheelers and three-wheelers.
7. Develop defensible IP only where genuine novelty is demonstrated.
8. Produce evidence suitable for partners, grant applications, and investor due diligence.

## Constraints

- **Prototype milestone:** 2026-11-12 (revisable only with documented justification).
- **Operating model:** software-led; outsourced manufacturing and independent lab testing as needed.
- **Funding ambition:** USD 1M — *not assumed available*; staged tranches against pass/fail gates.
- **Spending:** quotations before commitments; open-source tooling first.
- **Location:** India (research); remote/computational work may run anywhere.

## Non-goals (current stage)

- Claiming a novel or revolutionary architecture before evidence exists.
- Production tooling, volume procurement, or manufacturing capacity.
- Public disclosure of unpublished invention details.

## Success definition (prototype, v0 — revisable)

A physically tested machine on a 24–48 V bench that, within stated measurement uncertainty:

1. Rotates under closed-loop control across the agreed speed range.
2. Meets or exceeds a documented torque target at a documented current limit.
3. Produces a measured efficiency map (or at minimum efficiency at ≥3 operating points)
   traceable to calibrated instruments.
4. Runs a continuous-duty thermal test without exceeding insulation-class limits.
5. Yields data that either confirms or falsifies the primary research hypothesis.
