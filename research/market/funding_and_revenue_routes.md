# Funding and Revenue Routes — Project M1

**Status:** Research note, first pass
**Date:** 21 October 2026
**Scope:** Pre-revenue, zero-budget (D-009), unincorporated, solo-founder Indian R&D project developing a magnet-free 0.5 kW / 48 V / 3000 rpm BLDC-class motor (provisional target spec).
**Question answered:** Which routes can put money in the bank, and which are structurally closed.

**Confidence legend**

- **CONFIRMED** — read on an official government/institutional page or filing, date noted.
- **ESTIMATE** — my inference from a benchmark; not a published number.
- **CLOSED / UNVERIFIED** — cannot be relied on; do not plan around it.

---

## 0. Bottom line

There is no Indian scheme that funds the gap between "idea + simulation" and "working prototype" for an unincorporated solo founder. Every route that pays real money (TDB, EVolutionS, GENESIS, PLI) needs a registered company **and** a prototype at TRL 3–4 or above. The two schemes that accept individuals at idea/prototype stage (NIDHI-PRAYAS, ANIC) still need a host incubator or an open challenge cycle.

Therefore: **revenue before funding.** Paid design work is the only route producing cash inside 90 days. Grants should be treated as a second-order accelerator, not a funding source for Phase 1.

## (b) Revenue routes

Deal sizes marked ESTIMATE are derived from the Indian ER&D billing benchmark: Tata Elxsi FY2025-26 revenue **₹3,757.4 cr** against roughly **12,000** employees ≈ **₹31 lakh revenue per engineer-year** (gross, includes overhead and margin). A solo specialist billing direct should land well below that.

| Route | Buyer | Typical deal size | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Motor design services** (EM design, FEA, thermal, NVH, controller tuning) | 2W/3W OEMs, Tier-1 component makers, drone/agri-equipment firms, retrofit companies | ₹1.5–5 lakh per sub-task; ₹8–20 lakh per full motor design package (ESTIMATE) | Benchmark: Tata Elxsi FY26 revenue/headcount; Indian ER&D market demand documented in [WRI India EV transition study, Aug 2025](https://wri-india.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/The%20projected%20EV%20transition%20in%20the%20Indian%20automotive%20manufacturing%20industry%20online_0.pdf) |
| **Design audit / second opinion** on an existing supplier motor | OEMs unhappy with a Tier-1 design; investors doing technical DD | ₹75k–2 lakh, 1–2 weeks (ESTIMATE) | No public price data; fastest possible first invoice |
| **IP licensing to an OEM** | 2W/3W OEM or Tier-1 wanting a magnet-free option | Precedent: Bajaj Auto master licence — **$645,500** tech fee ($495,500 pre-production), 1,200 units to commercial production | CONFIRMED: [SEC EDGAR EX-10.7, 2011](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463101/000119312511055481/dex107.htm) |
| **Retrofit / conversion kit content** | Existing ARAI-certified retrofit kit makers | Kit retail **₹1.75–2.25 lakh**; M1 would sell the motor into the kit at ₹8–15k (ESTIMATE) | Konwert India Motors: KIM JET 180 ₹2.25 lakh, KIM DHOOR 120 ₹1.75 lakh; AIS-123 Part 3 (Amendment 4) governs electric propulsion kits; ARAI Annual Report 2024-25 |
| **Subcontract R&D under an institution-led grant** | IIT/NIT PI holding a DST TDP or TPEM project | ₹3–15 lakh subcontract (ESTIMATE) | DST TDP permits industry participation and prefers industry collaboration — [DST](https://dst.gov.in/technology-development-and-transfer) |
| **Low-volume specialty motor supply** | Agri-pump, cooling-fan, golf-cart, drone, small e-cart makers | ₹6–10k per unit at 50–500 units (ESTIMATE) | M1's 0.5 kW / 48 V envelope maps onto these, not onto road-legal traction |
| **Expert opinion / technical due diligence** | Angel investors, grant committees, OEM sourcing teams | ₹50k–1.5 lakh | No public data; sold on credentials, not product |

**Honest constraint on all of the above:** M1 currently has simulation and an envelope certification script, not measured hardware. Design services sold before any dyno data are sold on method, not proof. That is viable for audit/analysis work and not viable for "we will design your production motor."

## (c) Three fastest routes to money, ranked

**1. Paid design audit / analysis work — first invoice in 2–6 weeks.**
No grant cycle, no equity, no incorporation strictly required (proprietorship + GST invoicing suffices for B2B under ₹20 lakh/yr). Sell a bounded 1–2 week deliverable: independent FEA/thermal/NVH review of a supplier's motor, or a magnet-free feasibility study. Requires one credible sample report and a price. This is the only route that produces cash inside a quarter.

**2. Motor supply or design-for-kit into the retrofit channel — 1–3 months.**
The retrofit industry already has homologated kits and a regulatory path (AIS-123 Part 3); what it lacks is a domestic magnet-free motor. M1 sells a sub-component into an existing certified product instead of certifying a whole vehicle. Requires a physical sample and test data acceptable to the kit maker's ARAI file.

**3. NIDHI-PRAYAS via an empanelled PRAYAS Centre — ₹10 lakh, equity-free, 3–6 months.**
Lowest-competition meaningful grant for a hardware prototype, and it explicitly funds exactly the gap M1 has (design → functional prototype). Requires choosing a PC with motor/electrical lab capability, a 2-page proposal, and a working relationship with the incubator. Apply to several PCs in parallel.

**Not in the top three, deliberately:** ANIC (₹1 cr) has an unverified cycle and a competitive selection; TDB needs a company and a project report; GENESIS needs a pilot PO from a customer that does not yet exist.

## (d) Prerequisites and blockers

| Prerequisite | Why it blocks | Cost / time |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorporation (Pvt Ltd)** | TDB, GENESIS, EVolutionS, most incubator grants and all OEM POs require a legal entity. A proprietorship unlocks only services revenue. | MCA SPICe+ — ₹8–15k professional cost, 7–15 days (ESTIMATE) |
| **DPIIT startup recognition** | Required by GENESIS and most Startup India-linked schemes. Free, online. | Free, 1–3 weeks |
| **GST registration** | Needed to invoice B2B buyers credibly and mandatory above ₹20 lakh/yr services turnover. | Free, ~1 week |
| **Working prototype at TRL 3–4** | The gate for EVolutionS, ANIC, TDB and every accelerator. M1 is currently pre-TRL-3. | This is the actual funding gap — no scheme pays for it |
| **Test data (dyno, efficiency map, thermal)** | Without it, design services cannot be priced at the numbers in (b) and no OEM will license. | Dyno time bought or borrowed via an incubator |
| **Co-founder / team** | Grant panels and accelerators discount single-founder hardware projects; several schemes assume ≥2 promoters. Not a formal bar in most, but a practical one. | — |

**Hard blockers, stated plainly:**

- **PLI-Auto, PM E-DRIVE and all state EV capex subsidies are closed to M1.** They are manufacturing-scale or demand-side instruments. No amount of application effort changes this.
- **No grant funds the pre-prototype gap.** Every scheme in table (a) that pays ≥ ₹10 lakh requires either a prototype or a company, and most require both. The gap must be closed by service revenue or personal funds.
- **Grant timelines (4–9 months) exceed the project's runway.** A zero-budget project cannot wait for a disbursement cycle. Cash-flow sequencing, not scheme selection, is the binding constraint.
